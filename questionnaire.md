# Questionnaire Design and Scope

The final technical report describes 30 adapted assessment questions grouped into seven domains.
Use the full question wording from the project's questionnaire deliverable when available; this
outline mirrors the domains and question-number ranges documented in the report.

Domain 1: Governance and Risk Management
1. Does your organization maintain documented information security policies that are reviewed regularly?
2. Is responsibility for information security assigned to a specific person or team?
3. Does your organization regularly identify and assess cybersecurity risks related to the services it provides?
4. Does your organization identify and manage business and technology dependencies that could affect service security or availability?
Domain 2: Access Controls
5. Is each user provided with a unique account instead of sharing accounts?
6. Is multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for remote access and privileged accounts?
7. Are user permissions restricted to the minimum access needed for each person's job?
8. Are user access rights reviewed regularly and removed when employees leave or change roles?
Domain 3: Data Handling and Privacy
9. Is sensitive customer data encrypted during transmission and storage where appropriate?
10. Is access to customer data restricted to authorized personnel?
11. Are customer-data retention and secure-deletion procedures documented and followed?
12. Does your organization identify and comply with applicable privacy and data-protection obligations?
Domain 4: Incident Response
13. Does your organization maintain a documented cybersecurity incident response plan?
14. Are customers notified of security incidents affecting their data or services within agreed timeframes?
15. Are security incidents recorded, investigated, and reviewed to identify their causes?
16. Does your organization regularly test its incident response procedures?
Domain 5: Backups and Continuity
17. Are important systems and data backed up according to a documented schedule?
18. Are backups protected against unauthorized access, modification, or deletion?
19. Are backup restoration and recovery procedures tested regularly?
20. Are recovery time and recovery point objectives defined for important services?
Domain 6: Vulnerability Management
21. Does your organization regularly scan relevant systems and applications for known vulnerabilities?
22. Are vulnerabilities prioritized and fixed according to their severity and risk?
23. Are security tests performed periodically and after significant system changes?
24. Is software security patching managed through a documented process?
Domain 7: Subcontractors and Assurance Evidence
25. Does your organization identify subcontractors that can access customer data or support the service?
26. Are subcontractors assessed for cybersecurity risks before engagement?
27. Do subcontractor contracts include relevant security and data-protection requirements?
28. Can your organization provide relevant security assurance evidence, such as an in-scope SOC report, certification, or policy documentation, where available?
29. Are exceptions or security gaps identified in assurance reports reviewed and tracked for remediation?
30. Does your organization reassess cybersecurity risks when subcontractors, hosting arrangements, services, or security conditions change materially?
## Response options
- **Yes** — 2 points
- **Partially** — 1 point
- **No** — 0 points
- **Unknown** — 0 points; follow-up required
- **Not applicable** — exclude only when justified by service scope

**Formula:** Score (%) = (points earned ÷ maximum points for applicable questions) × 100.

The score bands in the report are illustrative and have not been empirically validated. Critical
weaknesses should be escalated regardless of the total score.
